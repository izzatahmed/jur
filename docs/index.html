QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Clarity at every step.

Is the University of Jordan operating this service now?

No. This is an independent technical demonstration prepared for institutional review. It is not an approved university service or an invitation to submit real confidential research.

Does submitting mean my manuscript will be published?

No. Submission is distinct from publication. Scientific certification requires independent human expert review, and public release requires an appropriate policy and author consent.

Who can see my uploaded file?

In demo mode nothing is uploaded. In configured live pilot mode, the file is routed by a secure backend to a private GitHub repository controlled by the portal administrator. Do not submit patient-identifiable information or confidential material.

Does artificial intelligence decide whether research is valid?

No. AI-assisted checks are advisory. Editors and independent human reviewers remain responsible for scientific validation and decisions.

Can I use this portal on a phone?

Yes. The interface is responsive and supports Arabic and English. The first version is intended as a pilot and demonstration.
